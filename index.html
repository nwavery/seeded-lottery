Your draw

Your text, character for character (as UTF-8, after Unicode normalization), is run through SHA-256. That hash seeds a xoshiro256** generator, which draws each ticket's numbers without bias. Same text, same hash, same numbers, on any device.

How to read these numbers

  • An average, not a forecast. ROI is the expected return on the ticket price: the average over every possible draw, not what any one ticket pays.
  • Tax applies to every prize. The annuity is taken at face value, undiscounted, which overstates 30 payments over 29 years.
  • Projected plays come from how much the lottery says the jackpot's cash value will grow (32.5% of each $2 Powerball play and 27.63% of each $5 Mega Millions play fund it). The lotteries project low: recent Powerball draws sold about 10% more.
  • Break-even jackpots hold sales at today's level; bigger jackpots sell more tickets and are shared more often, so the real break-even is higher.
  • Sharing. The chance of sharing assumes other players' numbers are random; real players favour birthdays, so popular numbers share more often.
  • California. In California every prize is pari-mutuel, so these fixed prizes don't apply there.
